ktor-batterypack example

Plant care, wallets and users.
One small app.

Identify plants from photos, follow their care schedule, and manage the users and wallets behind them.

Plants

Health, toxicity and care schedule for every plant.

Identify

Upload up to three photos to recognise a species.

Wallets

Check a balance and request a top-up.

Users

Create, look up, edit and delete users.
